Frequently asked questions

Is it real?

Yes. 518 homes in 239 Colorado communities, captured on site by one operator under commercial engagements, 2020 to 2026. Nothing synthetic, scraped or licensed in.

How is it different from an indoor scan dataset?

Indoor scan sets were captured by researchers at chosen viewpoints. This archive was captured for commercial work, one visit per house, with the raw brackets, log video and hand measured plans kept behind every delivery.

What's in the sample?

A full living room in every modality, native camera files. No cost, no NDA.

What's under the NDA?

Larger frozen evaluation slices, property disjoint, each with its manifest and fingerprints, to run on your own pipelines.

What about rights and privacy?

Every capture was shot for a commercial engagement, not for training. Single operator, never licensed. Rights settled at the source; chain of title closed. Identifiers are opaque, and location metadata is stripped and verified absent.

Where can I meet you?

CoRL 2026, Austin, November 9 to 12. The sample comes on a drive. Set a time.
